A community for mid-career people

For people who share the climb.

Ascend Together is a community for mid-career people who share the climb: tackling the steep sections, exploring new terrain and celebrating the summits.

For people building their careers, not starting them, whether employed or running their own business. People who want to keep growing, are as happy to give support as to receive it, and come for real conversation.
